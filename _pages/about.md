## Hello, I'm Yunho Jeong

I am a researcher at **TORIS**, where I work on RF signal processing and radar simulation for drone-detection systems.

![Yunho Jeong](/assets/images/2.jpg){: width="40%" height="40%" .align-center}

I received my master's degree in Interdisciplinary Studies of Artificial Intelligence from **Daegu Gyeongbuk Institute of Science and Technology (DGIST)** in 2026. My research background spans machine learning for biomedical time-series signals, computer vision, autonomous driving, and domain adaptation.
